About the Post

What is VFW Post 4651?

Nelson-Mack Narragansett VFW Post 4651 is a Veterans of Foreign Wars post at 7 Haven Avenue in Cranston. Our members are veterans who served overseas. We come together for fellowship, remembrance, service to the community and help for veterans and their families.

How did the Post get its name?

The Post was chartered on April 19, 1971. Two of its names belong to Rhode Island Marines killed in action in Vietnam: Private First Class George J. Mack of Cranston and Private First Class Daniel R. Nelson of Coventry. The third is the Narragansett Brewery, where the Post held its first meetings and where most of its original members worked.

We have been at home on Haven Avenue since 1986. Read the Post's history.

Who leads the Post?

Commander Garry Crum leads the Post with its elected officers. Meet the officers.

Membership

Who can join the VFW?

U.S. veterans who served honorably in an overseas war, campaign or operation recognized by the VFW, usually shown by a campaign medal or by receiving hostile fire or imminent danger pay. The national VFW sets the rules.

Not sure if you qualify? Bring your DD-214 or other proof of service and we'll help you check.

What does it cost to join?

The Post pays a new member's dues for the first year, so joining costs you nothing to start.

When and where are meetings?

Members meet on the last Wednesday of each month at 6:00 p.m. at the Post. Any change is posted on the Post calendar.

Can I come to a meeting before I join?

Yes. Come once, meet the members and see what we do before you decide. How to join.

Can family members get involved?

Yes. Relatives of eligible veterans may join the Auxiliary to VFW Post 4651, which supports veterans, military families and the Post's work in the community.

Help for veterans

Can the Post help with VA benefits or claims?

We can point you to help. The VFW's accredited Service Officers help veterans file VA claims free of charge. Call the Post and we'll connect you. Benefit clinics and other help are listed on the calendar when they're scheduled.

Is there housing at the Post?

There is a room for rent in the apartment upstairs. See the room and the details.

Visiting & the canteen

What is the canteen?

The canteen is the Post's bar and social room, with food, Sunday football and events. This week's games, the menu and regular prices are on the canteen page.

Are events open to the public?

Many are. Each listing on the calendar says who it's for. If you're not sure, call before you come.

Can I rent the hall?

Yes. The hall hosts birthdays, showers, repasts, meetings and classes. Call and press 2 for hall rentals. See the event spaces.

Getting involved

How can I help if I'm not a veteran?

Come to public events, volunteer, rent the hall, or offer time, skills or materials toward the hall's repairs. Neighbors and local businesses have already pitched in.

How do I stay up to date?

Follow the Post on Facebook and Instagram, or add the Post calendar to your phone.
