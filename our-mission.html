Our Mission

Camaraderie, service and advocacy, right here in Cranston.

Who we are

Nelson-Mack Narragansett Post 4651 is one of three VFW posts in Cranston, Rhode Island. We are an active social and mutual-aid club for veteran fellowship and citizenship, and our membership is made up largely of Post 9/11 veterans who welcome service members from every era.

In recent years the Post was close to closing. Veterans and neighbors stepped up to keep it open, and the hall on Haven Avenue is being rebuilt, repaired and reopened to the community.

The VFW mission

To foster camaraderie among United States veterans of overseas conflicts, to serve our veterans, the military and our communities, and to advocate on behalf of all veterans.

— Veterans of Foreign Wars of the United States

How we live it

Belong

Monthly meetings, cookouts, and a hall where you can just show up.

Serve

Honoring the fallen, supporting veterans and military families, and showing up for Cranston.

Organize

Advocacy and teamwork, from the Post up through the Rhode Island Department and VFW National.
