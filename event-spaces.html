The hall

Newly remodeled, with room for your event

Since the Post’s comeback began in 2024, the bar, the hall and all three bathrooms have been remodeled. The same rooms where members meet each month now host birthdays, showers, classes and community nights, just as they have for neighbors in Knightsville since 1986.

What people hold here

  • Birthday parties, baby showers and gender reveals
  • Weddings, receptions and family celebrations
  • Repasts and memorial gatherings
  • Business meetings and office parties
  • Paint nights, dance and step classes
  • Game-day watch parties and live music
  • Fundraisers, club meetings, and veteran and Auxiliary events

Why rent from the Post

Your event helps veterans

Post 4651 is a nonprofit veterans organization, and hall rentals help pay to keep the building open for the veterans and families who count on it. When you book with us, you’re supporting the Post that has served Cranston’s veterans since 1971.
