VFW Auxiliary

Family and friends of veterans, serving alongside Post 4651.

Who we are

The Auxiliary to Post 4651 is an active chapter made up of family members of eligible veterans, working alongside the Post on community service, fundraising, and support for veterans and military families.

Eligibility

Membership is open to spouses, widows/widowers, parents, children, grandchildren, siblings and other qualifying relatives of veterans who served in a war, campaign or expedition recognized by the VFW, as well as current and former members of the Armed Forces who qualify.
