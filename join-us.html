Membership

Post 4651 is an active social and mutual-aid club for veteran fellowship and citizenship. Most of our members are Post 9/11 veterans, and we welcome service members from all eras.

VFW membership is limited to those who meet national eligibility requirements, generally service overseas in a campaign or expedition, or in qualifying hostile-fire or imminent-danger pay conditions. Check the official list to see where you stand.

Not eligible for the VFW?

Family members and supporters can get involved through the VFW Auxiliary and the Sons of the VFW, and everyone is welcome at our public events and hall rentals. Ask us how.
