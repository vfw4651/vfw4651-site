How the Post Works

Who can join, how we decide things, and where the rules come from.

Where the rules come from

Every VFW Post follows the same rulebook. Congress chartered the Veterans of Foreign Wars in 1936. National's Bylaws and Manual of Procedure set out how each Post runs, and they are updated at the national convention every summer.

What follows is a plain-language summary in our own words. Where the national rules and this page differ, the national rules win. Section numbers are given so members can look them up.

Who can join

You can join the VFW if you served honorably in the U.S. Armed Forces, or are serving now, and one of these is true: (Bylaws 101)

  • You earned a campaign or expeditionary medal the VFW recognizes, a combat badge or ribbon (such as the Combat Action Ribbon or CIB), or the Purple Heart.
  • You received hostile fire pay or imminent danger pay.
  • You served in Korea for 30 consecutive days, or 60 days in all, after June 30, 1949.

You prove it with your DD-214 or other service records, and the Post checks it. VFW National's full eligibility list ↗

The Auxiliary

Spouses, parents, grandparents, brothers, sisters, children and grandchildren of anyone who is or was eligible for the VFW can join the Auxiliary to Post 4651. Veterans who are eligible for the VFW itself join the Post instead. (Bylaws 1102)

How joining works

  1. Say hello. Come to a meeting or the canteen, or send the interest form.
  2. Show your service. Bring your DD-214 or other proof. An officer checks it with you.
  3. Sign the application. You sign the official VFW membership application. (MOP 102)
  4. Review and vote. The Post's Reviewing Committee looks it over, and the members vote on it at a meeting. (MOP 103)
  5. Welcome aboard. You get your membership card and pin and are sworn in at a meeting.

Your first year's dues are covered by the Post.

Our officers

Members elect the Post's officers every year: (Bylaws 216)

  • Commander
  • Senior Vice Commander
  • Junior Vice Commander
  • Quartermaster (finances)
  • Three Trustees

The Commander appoints others, including the Adjutant (records and correspondence) and the Chaplain. Meet the current officers.

Our meetings

Members meet on the last Wednesday of each month at 6:00 p.m. at the Post. The VFW requires at least one meeting a month. (Bylaws 203)

  • Guests may attend with the Commander's OK. Call ahead or ask an officer.
  • Moving the regular meeting day takes 14 days' written notice to the members and the Department, then a vote. (MOP 205)
  • One-off changes go on the Post calendar.

Ground rules we live by

  • No politics. The Post endorses no candidate or party. We do speak up on laws that affect veterans. (Bylaws 702)
  • Your details stay private. Members' names, addresses and phone numbers are used only for Post business, never for political or commercial use. (Bylaws 714)
  • Fundraising is voted on first. The Post raises money only after the members vote for it. (MOP 214)
  • We remember. The Post marks Memorial Day, Veterans Day, Flag Day and Loyalty Day every year. (Bylaws 223)
