Post Officers

The members leading Post 4651.

Garry Crum

Commander

U.S. Marine Corps · Rhode Island Air National Guard · Iraq

Post member since 2023

Garry Crum is an Iraq War veteran who served in the Marine Corps and then the Rhode Island Air National Guard. A Cranston resident of more than 30 years, he has known this hall since his middle school days. He became Commander when the Post was about a week from closing, called an emergency meeting and wrote to former members to keep the doors open. Since then he has led the repairs to the hall and the rebuilding of the membership, which finished 2025–26 above 100 percent.

Bernard Brennan

Senior Vice Commander

U.S. Marine Corps · 2002–2013 · Iraq

Post member since 2025

Bernie Brennan, a Chicago native, joined the Marine Corps Reserve in 2002 and served eleven years, leaving as a sergeant. He deployed to Iraq twice with 2nd Battalion, 24th Marines, tracking the battalion’s wounded and fallen on the first tour, and later spent a year on the staff of the Wounded Warrior detachment at Naval Medical Center San Diego. A first-generation college graduate, he holds a Ph.D. and teaches political science at Johnson & Wales University. As Senior Vice Commander he runs the Post’s website and leads its membership effort.

Eddie Myers

Junior Vice Commander

U.S. Army · Iraq

Eddie Myers, who grew up in Dorchester in Boston, served in the U.S. Army and deployed to Iraq with the 25th Infantry Division. He is the Post’s Junior Vice Commander, and one of the people a newcomer can ask for at the door.

James Burnham

Quartermaster

U.S. Army · Iraq · Afghanistan

Post member since 2023

James Burnham served in the U.S. Army in both Iraq and Afghanistan and earned the Combat Infantryman Badge. As Quartermaster he looks after the Post’s finances and records.

Micholas Credle

Adjutant

U.S. Army

Micholas A. Credle is a retired United States Army Captain and decorated combat veteran who serves as Adjutant of VFW Post 4651. With more than ten years of military service, including leadership assignments at West Point and the United States Military Academy Preparatory School, he brings extensive experience in personnel management and organizational leadership. An advocate for veterans and military families, he is committed to veterans’ welfare, camaraderie, and community service.

Officers are elected by the members of Post 4651. VFW Post 4651 is a nonpartisan organization and does not endorse candidates for public office.
